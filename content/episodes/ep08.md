[[peter-norvig|Peter Norvig]] is the co-author of _Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach_ and a former director of research at Google. He is now a Distinguished Education Fellow at Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute and a researcher at Recursive, working on self-improving AI. His work spans AI research, software engineering, and education.
