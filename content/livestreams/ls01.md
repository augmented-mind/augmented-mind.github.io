We hosted [Caleb Ziems](https://x.com/cjziems), [Dora Zhao](https://x.com/dorazhao9), and [Diyi Yang](https://x.com/Diyi_Yang) for a live discussion of their new paper, _Reflections and New Directions for Human-Centered Large Language Models_.

Growing out of Stanford's CS329X Human-Centered LLM class, the paper distills 60+ pages of insights — spanning design, data sourcing, training, evaluation, and deployment — into a roadmap for building LLMs that humans can meaningfully live and work with.
