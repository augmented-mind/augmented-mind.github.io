**Caleb Ziems**, **Dora Zhao**, and **Diyi Yang** join us for a live stream exploring how a class collaboratively wrote a research paper on human-centered large language models.

This session dives into the process of large-scale collaborative research, the challenges of coordinating student contributions into a coherent academic paper, and the broader questions around human-centered approaches to LLM development.
