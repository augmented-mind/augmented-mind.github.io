**Caleb Ziems**, **Dora Zhao**, and **Diyi Yang** join us to discuss their new paper _"Reflections and New Directions for Human-Centered Large Language Models"_, a collaborative work from Diyi Yang's cs329x Human-Centered LLM class at Stanford. The paper presents 60+ pages of insights on developing AI that humans can meaningfully live and work with, spanning design and data sourcing to training, evaluation, and deployment.
