**Zora Wang** is a CS PhD student at **Carnegie Mellon University**, working at the intersection of coding agents and human-AI collaboration.

Her research explores how agents work, how humans work, and how the two can collaborate to get more done together.

## Appearances
- [[ep06|EP06: Skills, Memory, Collaboration: What's Next for AI Agents with Zora Wang]]

## Key Contributions
- **Agent Workflow Memory**: Inducing and reusing workflows so agents get more capable over long-horizon tasks.
- **Human vs. Agent Work**: Comparing how AI agents and humans carry out work across diverse occupations.
