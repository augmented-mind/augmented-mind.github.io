**Geoffrey Litt** is a design engineer at **Notion**. Previously, he was a senior researcher at the independent research lab **Ink & Switch**, and he earned his PhD in HCI at **MIT**.

His work focuses on malleable software and designing environments for thinking.

## Appearances
- [[ep07|EP07: Software with Taste with Geoffrey Litt]]

## Key Contributions
- **Malleable Software**: Designing tools that end users can adapt and reshape to fit their own needs.
- **Understanding is the New Bottleneck**: Arguing that comprehension, not code generation, is the emerging constraint in software.
