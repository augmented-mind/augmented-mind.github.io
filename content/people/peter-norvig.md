**Peter Norvig** is the co-author of **Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach** and a former director of research at **Google**. He is now a Distinguished Education Fellow at **Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute** and a researcher at **Recursive**, working on self-improving AI.

His work spans AI research, software engineering, and education.

## Appearances
- [[ep08|EP08: After Data and Compute, What's the Next Frontier in AI with Peter Norvig]]

## Key Contributions
- **Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach**: Co-authored one of the most widely used AI textbooks in university courses.
- **The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Data**: Argued that simple models trained on very large datasets often beat more elaborate models trained on less data.
- **Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years**: An essay on why real programming expertise comes from years of deliberate practice, not quick crash courses.
